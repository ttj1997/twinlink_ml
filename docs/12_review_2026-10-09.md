The reviews are source documents and are not translated or edited; the IDs below are those of the
reviews. IDs marked `*` are assigned here because the review lists the item without one.

**Stages.** 1 = can overturn core results; 2 = concerns statements about the method (or an
interpretation); 3 = correction (number, wording, reference, latent code defect without effect on a
reported result). **A1 and K1 are stage 1 by decision of the project lead (2026-10-09).** Every other
stage is a proposal of the filing session and open for the project lead's decision, except C1
(stage 1) and A2 (stage 2), set by the project lead on 2026-10-09.

**Status.** All findings except A1 and K1 are open. Nothing has been corrected in code or synthesis.
**A1 and K1 are confirmed** by checks on the stored solutions (Befund 78, 2026-10-09; artifacts
`review_a1_cascade_approach_m4c_lab`, `review_k1_solver_bracket`); the conclusion of Befund 77 is
suspended until a re-optimisation, and dated addenda to Befunde 73, 76 and 77 refer to Befund 78. The
items of R1 that are instances of K1 (X, F2.1, F3.1) are confirmed with it. Corrected in `src/` (Befund 79, 2026-10-09):
K1 by a fallback bracket, A1 by the check along the exchanger (legacy mode `warm_end`); M1 of
Befund 69 re-computed (Befund 80); the coupling test is to be re-fixed (draft
